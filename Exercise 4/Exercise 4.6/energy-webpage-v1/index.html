The SVG Modern Cottage

An exploration of Scalable Vector Graphics, nested grouping, and coordinate systems.

📏

Technical Coordinate Mapping

The table below annotates each SVG shape type used in the "Garden & Yellow Cottage" diagram.

Shape Type Code Element Key Attributes Visual Part
Rectangle <rect x="250" y="230" ...> Top-left corner at (250, 230). Extends 300px right and 220px down. Main House Body
Circle <circle cx="415" cy="395" r="3"> Center point at (415, 395) with a radius of 3px. Door Knob
Ellipse <ellipse cx="100" cy="80" rx="40" ry="25"> Center at (100, 80). Horizontal radius 40px, vertical radius 25px. Cloud Shapes
Line <line x1="0" y1="0" x2="0" y2="-40"> Starts at origin (0, 0) and ends at (0, -40) relative to its group. Flower Stems
Path (Polygon) <path d="M 230 230 L 400 80 L 570 230 Z"> M moves to (230, 230). L draws lines to (400, 80) and (570, 230). Z closes the triangle. Roof Triangle
Path (Curve) <path d="M 400 600 Q 380 520 400 450"> M starts at (400, 600). Q draws a Quadratic Bézier curve through control point (380, 520). Stone Path
Text <text x="400" y="580"> Baseline positioned at (400, 580). text-anchor="middle" centers the text. Diagram Label
Group (<g>) <g fill="#e0f2fe" stroke="#0ea5e9"> Wraps window elements. fill and stroke apply to all children automatically. Window Units
